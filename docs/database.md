# Database

The data model and the guarantees the database enforces (constraints, triggers, RLS, RPCs), with the app modules that front them. Domain words are defined in `GLOSSARY.md`; decisions in `docs/adr/`.

## Slots and bookings

- A slot is one instructor × one hour of a schedule day with a seat limit (`schedule_slots.seats`, ADR-0002); `hour` is a `smallint`, working hours are the half-open range `[open_hour, close_hour)` — 10–16 means slots 10…15. A rider books a seat with an instructor (`bookings.instructor_id`); the horse (`bookings.horse_id`) is optional and only on an active booking — the stable assigns it.
- The seat limit is the trigger `bookings_seat_limit`, not application code: it locks the slot row (`FOR UPDATE`) and refuses a booking over the limit with `SB009`; a missing slot or day surfaces as `23503`, "outside working hours" as `23514`. One pending-or-active booking per rider per hour is the partial unique index `bookings_rider_hour_key` (`23505`); one active booking per assigned horse per hour is `bookings_horse_hour_key`. Trigger `bookings_cancellation` makes a cancelled booking final, lets each role write only itself into `cancelled_by` (`42501`) and clears the horse. Only the owner or the instructor of the booking's slot changes `horse_id` (trigger `bookings_horse_assignment`, `42501` otherwise; no time threshold) — the owner by a plain UPDATE under RLS, an instructor only through `public.assign_my_slot_horse(booking, horse)`, which writes nothing but the horse (horses to pick from, with a „zajęty” flag and no rider data: `public.get_my_slot_horse_options(from)`); the "slot already started" threshold for cancelling — by either role — lives in the endpoints. Rider-facing messages live in `src/lib/bookings/errors.ts`; stable-facing schedule codes (`SB001`–`SB011`) in `src/lib/schedule/errors.ts`; horse-assignment refusals in `src/lib/bookings/stable-errors.ts`.

## Schedule

- The schedule (day, horses, instructors, seat limits) is written only by `public.save_schedule_day`, in one transaction; direct INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE on `schedule_days`, `schedule_day_horses`, `schedule_day_instructors` and `schedule_slots` is revoked (`42501`). Seeds and demo data write as `postgres` and must keep the seat-sum rule themselves.

## Anonymised riders

- `bookings.rider_id` is nullable: NULL means an anonymised rider (ADR-0001). Deleting a profile first deletes all of the rider's bookings whose slot hour has not ended (trigger `profiles_release_future_bookings`, boundary `private.booking_slot_ended` in `Europe/Warsaw`), then the FK sets `rider_id` to NULL on the rest. Any code that shows a rider next to a booking handles the missing rider.

## Accounts, instructors and the stable context

- Account role is `profiles.role` in (`instructor`, `rider`), immutable. An instructor account is linked to at most one instructor (`instructors.account_id`, unique, never writable by `authenticated` and not readable by `anon`/`authenticated` either — SELECT is granted on the other columns only, so `select=*` or a filter on `account_id` fails with `42501`). The account's own instructor comes from `public.my_instructor()` (`getOwnInstructor`), which instructors of the owner's stable have an account (and which is the owner) from `public.list_instructor_accounts()` (`listInstructorAccounts`). The stable context is `private.current_stable_id()` (stable of the account's ACTIVE instructor); everything a stable does — schedule, horses, instructors, bookings, horse assignment, cancellation, statistics — requires the owner, `private.owned_stable_id()` (`private.is_stable_owner()`, `private.current_instructor_id()` alongside); the one exception is the schedule, whose stable is `private.schedule_stable_id()` (owner's stable, or that of an ACTIVE instructor with the schedule permission; `public.schedule_stable_id()` / `getScheduleStableId` for the app) — `save_schedule_day` and the SELECT policies the schedule form needs (schedule days, day horses, day instructors, slots, the stable's horses and instructors) and `get_slot_occupancy` use it. A stable is created only by `public.create_stable(name, city, instructor_name)` together with its owner instructor (direct INSERT on `stables` is revoked); the owner cannot be deactivated (`SB012`). `cancelled_by = 'stable'` still means "cancelled by the stable", i.e. its owner. An instructor account that is not the owner reads only its own slots, their schedule days, its own instructor row and stable name, and ACTIVE bookings on its slots; rider names and the assigned horse come from `public.get_my_slot_bookings(from)` (RLS on `profiles` is not loosened), and for its month summary (`/instruktor/podsumowanie`, tab „Podsumowanie miesiąca” of every active instructor with an account) from `public.get_my_month_bookings(from, to)` with a rider key (the account; NULL plus `anonymised` for an anonymised rider) and `boarder` (#118: `private.is_boarder_in_month` for the ride's month — the summary's „Pensjonariusze” section); it assigns horses on its own slots in „Moje sloty” (`POST /api/bookings/instructor-assign-horse`). Riders and the owner keep the open catalogue read (`private.reads_catalogue()`). The schedule permission (GLOSSARY „Uprawnienie do układania grafiku”) is `instructors.arranges_schedule` — like `account_id` neither readable nor writable by `authenticated`; the owner reads it in `list_instructor_accounts()`, the account its own in `my_instructor()`, and only the owner changes it through `public.set_schedule_permission(instructor, granted)` (`42501` not an owner, `SB013` foreign instructor, `SB023` no account, `SB024` inactive, `SB025` the owner himself; messages `schedulePermissionErrorMessage`). CHECK `instructors_schedule_permission_standing_check` keeps it on active instructors with an account only; trigger `instructors_lost_standing_drops_schedule_permission` clears it on deactivation or account loss, reactivation does not restore it. Deactivating an instructor empties that context, so its account reads nothing — no separate logic; reactivation restores it without a new invitation. Routing: owner → `/osrodek`, instructor → `/instruktor` („Moje sloty”); an instructor with the schedule permission (standing `arranging-instructor`) also gets the „Grafik” tab `/instruktor/grafik` (`instructorTabs`, component `InstructorTabs.astro`; same `ScheduleDayPage.astro` and `POST /api/schedule/save` as the owner, return route `scheduleRouteFor`; refused `42501` → `/instruktor?error=` „Nie masz już uprawnienia do układania grafiku”, `scheduleErrorMessage(code, detail, caller)`), without it that path redirects to „Moje sloty”; deactivated instructor → `/instruktor/konto-nieaktywne` from every protected route (`my_instructor()` still returns its instructor with `active = false`), decided by `instructorStandingOf` / `instructorRedirect` in `src/lib/auth/roles.ts` (`locals.instructorStanding`).

## Instructor invitations

- Instructor invitations (`instructor_invitations`) are closed to `authenticated`; the owner works through `public.issue_instructor_invitation` / `revoke_instructor_invitation` / `list_instructor_invitations` (refusals `42501`, `SB013` foreign instructor, `SB014` inactive, `SB015` already has an account, `23514` bad address). Only `sha256` of the token is stored; the token travels once, in the link `/auth/signup?zaproszenie=<64 hex>`. Deactivating an instructor revokes its open invitation in the same transaction (trigger `instructors_deactivation_revokes_invitation`), so reactivation never revives an old link. The sign-up page reads it anonymously through `public.invitation_by_token` (stable name, address, state). Acceptance happens inside account creation: sign-up metadata `invitation_token` makes the trigger `handle_new_user` create an `instructor` profile, link the invitation's instructor and set `accepted_at` in GoTrue's transaction, or abort the sign-up (`SB016` unknown token, `SB017` expired, `SB018` revoked, `SB019` used, `SB020` other address, `SB021` instructor inactive — defensive, deactivation already revoked the link, `SB022` instructor has an account). GoTrue hides that code behind a 500, so `/api/auth/signup` takes the reason from the invitation's state (`signupInvitation` in `src/lib/auth/invitation.ts`) and signs up with the invitation's address, never the form's.

## Rates, extra costs and settlements

- Rates (GLOSSARY „Stawka”, spec #93) are `public.rates`: one row per change, `rider_id` NULL = stable rate (always with an amount), otherwise an individual rate whose NULL amount means "back to the stable rate"; `from_month` is the first day of the month, `amount` integer grosze 0..1 000 000, one change per (stable, rider-or-none, month) (`rates_one_change_per_month_key`, NULLS NOT DISTINCT — upsert overwrites). Owner-only RLS (`private.owned_stable_id()`, individual rates only for `private.is_rider_of_my_stable`). Trigger `rates_month_rules`, also for `postgres`: a change only from the current or next month (`private.warsaw_month_start()`, `SB026`), only a planned change can be deleted (`SB027`; FK cascades pass), key columns immutable (`SB028`). Which rate applies in a month is decided by `src/lib/settlements/rates.ts` (first stable rate applies backwards); amounts are computed only in `src/lib/settlements/settlements.ts` and printed by `formatMoney`. Extra costs (GLOSSARY „Koszt dodatkowy”, #96) are `public.extra_costs`: stable, rider (FK cascade), `month` (first day), `name` 1–80 chars trimmed, `amount` integer grosze 1..10 000 000; owner-only RLS, writes only for `private.is_rider_of_my_stable`; trigger `extra_costs_month_rules` (also `postgres`) refuses a future month (`SB029`); only `name` and `amount` are updatable (column grant). „Do zapłaty” = rides amount + costs (`riderAmounts`); extra costs are added only on the rider settlement (cost forms always return there, `costFormEndpoint`, which refuses a form without a return rider before any write); the settlements page lists the stable's other riders — the `rider` profiles the owner can read (`listStableRiders`) minus the month's list — under „Pozostali jeźdźcy ośrodka” (`SettlementsView.others`, #106), each linking to their rider settlement. Rate kind (#117): `rates.kind` `recreation` | `training` (immutable), part of `rates_one_change_per_month_key`; a boarder in the month (`riderKindOf` in `src/lib/boarding/month.ts`, rider kind `recreation` | `boarder`; rate kind `RATE_KIND_OF` / `riderRateKindIn`) pays rides × training rate (an individual rate saved for a boarder is a training rate — the server derives the kind), plus the boarding fee (GLOSSARY „Opłata za pensjonat”) of each horse whose stay overlaps the month: `public.boarding_fees` (horse, stable — FK to `horse_owners`, cascade; `from_month`, `amount` 0..10 000 000; one change per horse and month), owner-only writes, the boarder reads his own; trigger `boarding_fees_month_rules` (also `postgres`): `SB060` month, `SB061` deleting a fee in force, `SB062` key columns. Which fee applies is `src/lib/boarding/fees.ts` (first fee applies backwards); a missing training rate or fee means no „Do zapłaty”. The settlements list and the owner/instructor month summary come in two sections, „Rekreacja” and „Pensjonariusze” (#118, `SettlementList.sections` / `MonthSummaryView.sections`, rule „pensjonariusz w miesiącu”); a boarder whose horse's stay overlaps the month has a card also without rides; a section sum exists only when every card in it has „Do zapłaty”, and „Razem” and the panel sum only when every section has its sum — never a partial sum (#112; the panel then asks „Ustaw stawkę” or „Ustaw opłatę za pensjonat”). An individual rate's kind is the rider's kind in the month it starts (`riderRateStart`).

## Horses and boarding

- Horses (#114, ADR 0005) have `horses.kind` (`stable` = Koń ośrodka, `boarding` = Koń pensjonatowy; immutable). The boarding horse's owner and stays live outside the open horse catalogue, in `public.horse_owners` (rider FK `on delete set null`) and `public.horse_stays` (no overlap per horse — exclusion constraint), readable only by the stable owner and the boarder, written only through `public.add_boarding_horse(name, email, arrival)`, `add_horse_stay`, `set_stay_departure` (either side; `departure_set_by`; not after today, not before arrival nor the horse's last ride) and read for the owner's list by `get_stable_boarding_horses()`; rider lookup by e-mail `public.find_rider_by_email` (owner only, `SB040`). Codes `SB040`–`SB047`, messages `src/lib/boarding/errors.ts`. A boarding horse is never a working horse (trigger on `schedule_day_horses`, `SB046`). Deleting a boarder's profile is never refused: trigger `profiles_end_horse_stays` (#120) ends the open stays of their horses in every stable on the deletion day (`departure_set_by = 'account_deleted'`, Europe/Warsaw) and deletes stays that have not started yet; the FK leaves the horses ownerless — `get_stable_boarding_horses()` still returns them (a horse without any stay as one row with NULL stay columns) and „Pensjonariusze” lists them under „Konie bez właściciela” (#112). A booking on a training slot always keeps the boarder's horse: clearing it is `SB063` (trigger `bookings_slot_rules`, #112), so the owner's and instructor's horse select has no „bez konia” there. „Pensjonariusz w miesiącu” is `private.is_boarder_in_month` / `isBoarderInMonth` (`src/lib/boarding/month.ts`) — keep both in step (mirrored case tables in `month.test.ts` and `supabase/tests/boarding_horses.sql`, `stayCoversDay` ↔ `private.stay_covers_day` in `training_slots.sql` step 20). App code reads boarding horses with owners and stays only through `listBoardingHorses` (`src/lib/boarding/queries.ts`) and applies the rule only through `horsesOf` / `isBoarderWith` / `horsesInMonth` / `horsesOnDay` / `riderKindOf` (#112); stable endpoints share one gate, `stableRequest(context, scope, back, caller)` (`src/lib/stables/owner-request.ts`; `ownerRequest` for owner endpoints, `"booking-decider"` — owner or slot instructor, decided by the DB — for accept/reject). `private.is_rider_of_my_stable` also covers a boarder from the horse's arrival day.

## Booking states, acceptance, rejection and notifications

- Booking states (#122, ADR 0006): `bookings.status` is `pending` | `active` | `rejected` | `cancelled`; „wygasły” is derived on read (`bookingStateAt` — a pending booking whose slot has started). Only `active` takes a seat: every TS read that counts or shows seats and rides filters through `isActiveBooking` (`src/lib/bookings/status.ts`, used by `isRide`), SQL reads by `status = 'active'`. `bookings.accepted_at` marks a booking that was accepted (stamped by trigger `bookings_acceptance` when a booking becomes active; CHECK `bookings_accepted_at_consistency_check`); cancellation statistics count only `isCancellationOfAccepted` (`StatsBooking.wasAccepted`), and „jeździec ośrodka” (`private.is_rider_of_my_stable`) needs a booking with `accepted_at` — pending, rejected or a withdrawn pending booking does not count. Seed and demo insert bookings with an explicit status. Acceptance (#124, migration `20261209090000_booking_acceptance.sql`): a rider's booking is always created `pending` (column default, INSERT policy); the booking rules (`bookings_seat_limit`, `bookings_slot_rules`) also run for pending — a pending booking on a full slot is `SB009` — but only active counts. Trigger `bookings_state_machine` (only for `authenticated`; DB functions and `postgres` bypass it) allows an app status change only to `cancelled` — the rider from pending or active, the stable from active — refuses everything else with `42501` and an app booking on a started slot with `SB064` (`private.booking_slot_started`, twin of `hasSlotStarted`). The owner accepts only through `public.accept_booking(booking)` (`acceptBooking`; `SB064` started, `SB065` not pending, `SB066` not the owner's booking, `SB009` over the limit — the seat trigger's slot lock serialises concurrent accepts; it returns the booking's resulting status, which the endpoint checks before `sukces=`; one refusal table for acceptance and rejection, `decisionRefusal` in `src/lib/bookings/decisions.ts`) from „Zapisy do przyjęcia” `/osrodek/zapisy-do-przyjecia` (`POST /api/bookings/accept`), listed by `public.get_pending_bookings()` (rider name and e-mail — RLS on `profiles` does not give a pending-only rider) and composed by `composePendingBookings` (drops expired). e2e scenarios that need an active rider booking accept it with `acceptPendingBooking` (`e2e/helpers.ts`). Rejection (#125, migration `20261210090000_booking_rejection.sql`): a rejected booking carries `rejection_reason` (1–300 chars, trimmed, shown to the rider as „Odrzucony: <powód>” in „Moje zapisy” until the slot ends, then in history), `rejection_source` (`stable` | `no_seats` | `schedule_change`) and `rejected_at` (CHECK `bookings_rejection_consistency_check`); every rejection goes through `private.reject_bookings(ids, source, reason)` — the owner through `public.reject_booking(booking, reason)` (`rejectBooking`, `POST /api/bookings/reject`, form `RejectBookingForm.astro`; `SB070` no reason, `SB071` too long, `SB064`–`SB066` as for acceptance; messages `src/lib/bookings/decisions.ts`), and `accept_booking`, which locks the slot before the booking and, when the acceptance fills the slot, rejects the slot's other pending bookings „Brak miejsc” (`private.reject_pending_of_full_slot`) in the same transaction. `get_pending_bookings().rejected_before` flags a rider the stable already rejected by hand on that slot. Notifications (#129, migration `20261214090000_booking_notifications.sql`): `public.notifications` (recipient, kind, booking, reason) is written only by trigger `bookings_notifications` on every booking state change, in the same transaction and whatever the path — new pending and rider-cancelled active go to the stable (owner + the slot instructor's account, `private.booking_stable_recipients`), accepted / rejected (with reason) / stable-cancelled active to the rider; withdrawing a pending booking notifies nobody. Expiry is derived on read. Read through `public.get_my_notifications()`; unread = newer than `notification_views.seen_at`, set by `public.mark_notifications_seen()` when „Powiadomienia” (`/powiadomienia`, every role) opens. The list and the bell count are composed only by `composeNotifications` / `countUnread` (`src/lib/notifications/`); the bell is `NotificationBell.astro` in the shared `Topbar`.

## Occupancy reads

- Riders cannot SELECT other riders' bookings (RLS). Slot occupancy comes from the RPC `public.get_slot_occupancy(stable_id, day)` — `security definer`, returns only `(instructor_id, hour, taken)`. Wrapper in `src/lib/bookings/queries.ts`. Place occupancy (GLOSSARY „Zajętość placów”, #116) is `public.get_place_occupancy(stable_id, day)` — same idea, rows `(place_id, hour, instructor_id, instructor_name, kind, taken)` for slots with seats or active bookings; only the stable owner and a boarder whose horse's stay covers the day, otherwise `SB054`. One view model `composePlaceOccupancy` (`src/lib/place-occupancy/`) serves `/jezdziec/zajetosc-placow` and `/osrodek/zajetosc-placow`.
